# Metric cards

One card per registered metric, generated by `scripts/build_metric_cards.py`.

- [`alignment`](alignment.md): Alignment: mean distance between L2-normalized views of the same clip, to the power alpha.
- [`alpha_req`](alpha_req.md): Power-law decay exponent of the covariance eigenspectrum (alpha-ReQ).
- [`anisotropy`](anisotropy.md): Spectral anisotropy of the centered, row-normalized matrix.
- [`anisotropy/cosine`](anisotropy__cosine.md): Mean pairwise cosine similarity between distinct samples.
- [`cls_patch_cosine`](cls_patch_cosine.md): Mean cosine between the class token(s) and the patch tokens of one clip.
- [`dime`](dime.md): DiME: permuted minus paired matrix-based joint entropy of two views.
- [`effective_rank`](effective_rank.md): Effective rank: exponential of the Shannon entropy of the normalized spectrum.
- [`eigenvalue_early_enrichment`](eigenvalue_early_enrichment.md): Top-heaviness of the covariance spectrum over the ambient dimension (EEE).
- [`embedding_norm`](embedding_norm.md): Mean Euclidean norm of the representations, with its spread.
- [`gaussianity`](gaussianity.md): Epps-Pulley distance from an isotropic Gaussian along random directions.
- [`gaussianity/ks`](gaussianity__ks.md): Kolmogorov-Smirnov variant of gaussianity.
- [`gaussianity/swd`](gaussianity__swd.md): Sliced-Wasserstein shape distance from an isotropic Gaussian (VISReg); standardizes internally, so no registry preprocessing.
- [`infonce`](infonce.md): Full-batch InfoNCE loss between two views of the same clips.
- [`information_imbalance`](information_imbalance.md): Information imbalance from representation A to representation B.
- [`intrinsic_dimension`](intrinsic_dimension.md): TwoNN intrinsic dimension (Facco et al.).
- [`intrinsic_dimension/gride`](intrinsic_dimension__gride.md): GRIDE intrinsic dimension at the 8th-neighbor scale.
- [`intrinsic_dimension/mle`](intrinsic_dimension__mle.md): Levina-Bickel MLE intrinsic dimension, k = 10..20.
- [`lidar`](lidar.md): LiDAR: effective rank of the LDA matrix with clips as classes and views as samples.
- [`local_rectifiability`](local_rectifiability.md): UR-JEPA beta-number flatness around an n-plane across dyadic scales (Eq. 24).
- [`matrix_entropy`](matrix_entropy.md): Matrix-based Renyi entropy of the trace-normalized Gram matrix.
- [`mlid`](mlid.md): Geometric mean of per-point local intrinsic dimension (mLID).
- [`mst_dimension`](mst_dimension.md): Intrinsic dimension from the scaling of minimum-spanning-tree length.
- [`neighborhood_curvature`](neighborhood_curvature.md): Local bending of the cloud: mean cosine between unit edges to the k nearest neighbors.
- [`neighborhood_overlap`](neighborhood_overlap.md): Neighborhood overlap: mean fraction of k nearest neighbors shared by two representations of the same items.
- [`normalized_std`](normalized_std.md): Mean per-channel standard deviation of the L2-normalized output.
- [`participation_ratio`](participation_ratio.md): Participation ratio of the covariance eigenvalues, (sum lambda)^2 / sum lambda^2.
- [`participation_ratio/corrected`](participation_ratio__corrected.md): Row-bias-corrected participation ratio of Chun et al. (2026) on the raw matrix.
- [`pte`](pte.md): Pitch-transposition equivariance, linear probe, phase distance (Kanatas et al., 2026).
- [`pte/mlp`](pte__mlp.md): Pitch-transposition equivariance with an MLP probe.
- [`self_clustering`](self_clustering.md): Self-clustering score: excess squared cosine over a uniform spherical cloud.
- [`sparsity`](sparsity.md): Fraction of active entries and a Hoyer-type l1/l2 density ratio.
- [`spectral_entropy`](spectral_entropy.md): Shannon entropy of the normalized spectrum of the centered covariance.
- [`token_cosine`](token_cosine.md): Mean cosine similarity between distinct tokens of one clip.
- [`token_gram_drift`](token_gram_drift.md): Squared Frobenius drift of a clip's token cosine Gram matrix against a reference field.
- [`token_norm_outliers`](token_norm_outliers.md): Fraction of high-norm tokens, and how concentrated the largest token is in a few channels.
- [`trajectory_curvature`](trajectory_curvature.md): Mean turning angle of a frame trajectory, oriented angle in [0, pi].
- [`uniformity`](uniformity.md): Uniformity: log average pairwise Gaussian potential on the unit sphere.
