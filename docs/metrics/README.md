# Metric cards

One card per registered metric, generated by `scripts/build_metric_cards.py`.

- [`alignment`](alignment.md): Alignment: mean distance between L2-normalized views of the same sample, to the power alpha.
- [`alpha_req`](alpha_req.md): Power-law decay exponent of the covariance eigenspectrum (alpha-ReQ).
- [`anisotropy`](anisotropy.md): Spectral anisotropy of the centered, row-normalized matrix.
- [`cka`](cka.md): Linear centered kernel alignment between two representations of the same items (Kornblith et al., 2019).
- [`cls_patch_cosine`](cls_patch_cosine.md): Mean cosine between the class token(s) and the patch tokens of one sample.
- [`cluster_quality`](cluster_quality.md): k-means Davies-Bouldin index, with the inertia in the extras (Whetten et al., 2025).
- [`cosine_anisotropy`](cosine_anisotropy.md): Cosine anisotropy: mean cosine similarity between distinct samples.
- [`cycle_knn`](cycle_knn.md): Fraction of items that are a nearest neighbor in A of one of their nearest neighbors in B (Huh et al., 2024); the ordering matters.
- [`dime`](dime.md): DiME: permuted minus paired matrix-based joint entropy of two views.
- [`effective_rank`](effective_rank.md): Effective rank: exponential of the Shannon entropy of the normalized spectrum.
- [`eigenvalue_early_enrichment`](eigenvalue_early_enrichment.md): Top-heaviness of the covariance spectrum over the ambient dimension (EEE).
- [`embedding_norm`](embedding_norm.md): Mean Euclidean norm of the representations.
- [`gaussianity`](gaussianity.md): Distance from an isotropic Gaussian along random directions (Epps-Pulley, KS or VISReg shape).
- [`infonce`](infonce.md): InfoNCE loss between augmented views of the same samples.
- [`information_imbalance`](information_imbalance.md): Information imbalance from representation A to representation B.
- [`intrinsic_dimension/gride`](intrinsic_dimension__gride.md): GRIDE intrinsic dimension at the 8th-neighbor scale.
- [`intrinsic_dimension/mle`](intrinsic_dimension__mle.md): Levina-Bickel MLE intrinsic dimension, k = 10..20.
- [`intrinsic_dimension/mlid`](intrinsic_dimension__mlid.md): Geometric mean of per-point local intrinsic dimension (mLID).
- [`intrinsic_dimension/mst`](intrinsic_dimension__mst.md): Intrinsic dimension from the growth of minimum-spanning-tree length.
- [`intrinsic_dimension/twonn`](intrinsic_dimension__twonn.md): TwoNN intrinsic dimension (Facco et al.).
- [`jacobian_effective_rank`](jacobian_effective_rank.md): Effective rank of a layer readout's input-output Jacobian (Chung and Kim, 2026).
- [`lidar`](lidar.md): LiDAR: effective rank of the LDA matrix, with samples as classes and their views as within-class points.
- [`local_rectifiability`](local_rectifiability.md): UR-JEPA beta-number flatness around an n-plane across dyadic scales (Eq. 24).
- [`matrix_entropy`](matrix_entropy.md): Matrix-based Renyi entropy of the trace-normalized Gram matrix.
- [`neighborhood_curvature`](neighborhood_curvature.md): Neighborhood curvature: mean cosine between the unit edges to the k nearest neighbors.
- [`neighborhood_overlap`](neighborhood_overlap.md): Neighborhood overlap: mean fraction of the k nearest neighbors shared by two representations.
- [`normalized_std`](normalized_std.md): Mean per-channel standard deviation of the L2-normalized output.
- [`participation_ratio`](participation_ratio.md): Participation ratio (sum lambda)^2 / sum lambda^2 of the covariance eigenvalues.
- [`pte`](pte.md): Pitch-transposition equivariance, linear probe, phase distance (Kanatas et al., 2026).
- [`rsa`](rsa.md): Spearman correlation of the pairwise-distance vectors of two representations (Kriegeskorte et al., 2008).
- [`self_clustering`](self_clustering.md): Self-clustering score: excess squared cosine over a uniform spherical cloud.
- [`sparsity`](sparsity.md): Sparsity: Hoyer-type l1/l2 density and the fraction of active entries.
- [`svcca`](svcca.md): Mean canonical correlation of the leading SVD directions of two representations (Raghu et al., 2017).
- [`token_cosine`](token_cosine.md): Mean cosine similarity between distinct tokens of one sample.
- [`token_gram_drift`](token_gram_drift.md): Squared Frobenius drift of a sample's token cosine Gram matrix against a reference field.
- [`token_norm_outliers`](token_norm_outliers.md): Fraction of high-norm tokens, and the channel concentration of the largest one.
- [`trajectory_curvature`](trajectory_curvature.md): Mean turning angle of a frame trajectory, oriented angle in [0, pi].
- [`uniformity`](uniformity.md): Uniformity: log mean pairwise Gaussian potential on the unit sphere.
